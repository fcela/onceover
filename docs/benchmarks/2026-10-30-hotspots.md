# Hotspots detector

Date: 2026-10-30. Issue #58: single-column concentration
findings cover marginal shares, but a cell of a column
combination can hold far more rows than the marginals predict
while every marginal looks unremarkable. This change adds the
`hotspots` detector (onceover-mines, over the shared encoded
matrix).

## Method

Eligible features are the encoded matrix's nominal
(level-coded) and numeric (quantile-binned) features with
2–20 levels/bins. For every pair — and, at deep budget, every
triple within the cell caps (1,024 pair cells, 4,096 triple
cells, at most 400 triples) — each cell's observed count over
the combination's complete rows is compared with the
independence baseline from the margins. A cell is reported
only when it clears all of:

- expected rows under independence >= 10,
- observed / expected ratio >= 2,
- observed rows >= 20 and share of scanned rows > 0.5%,
- a standardized residual z = (O − E)/√E above a Bonferroni
  bar over **all examined cells** at family alpha 0.01 — the
  multiplicity burden is computed, stated in the evidence
  (`cells_examined`, `z_bar`), and priced into every report.

At most three cells per combination and fifteen findings per
profile (twenty at deep). Counts are over the encoded row
sample (standard cap 20,000 rows); the coverage entry and the
evidence both say so. Finding type `hotspot`, category
`concentration`; the payload names the columns, the cell's
level or bin range per column, observed and expected rows,
the ratio, and the z statistics.

## Verification

- Planted fixture (also tests/test_hotspots.py): a 10,000-row
  frame whose (a1, b1) cell holds 1,000 rows against ~100
  expected under its 10%/10% marginals reports the cell at
  ratio 10 with the baseline in the evidence. An independent
  frame with the same marginals produces no hotspot finding.
- Battery: two cases added — `concentration-hotspot-cell`
  passes, `concentration-hotspot-null` passes (no hotspot
  finding under independence). On this branch's build the
  battery reads 50 pass / 3 fail / 0 gap / 9 observed of 62
  cases: the two standing single-binary drift fails, plus
  `missingness-sentinel-values`, which needs the sentinels
  detector from PR #77 (not in this branch) and passes on
  that build. No other case changed status.
- Triples: a planted 3-column cell reports at deep budget;
  the standard budget reports pairs only.
- Corpus: expectations unchanged.
