# Null bars

Issue #374, step 3. Several passes computed a statistic that compares a statement with chance, and then used it only to rank. This step makes those statistics conditions for stating anything. Two statement forms whose numbers carry no information are removed. The bars of #361–#364 (breaks, regimes, covering rules) are untouched.

## Changes

| pass | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| approximate FDs (`fd.rs`) | accepted at ≤ 1% violations; the reliable fraction of information (`rfi`, observed minus chance-expected) only capped the score | stated only with `rfi ≥ 0.25` (`fd::MIN_RFI`); the others are counted in coverage |
| rollups and duplicate encodings (`dupe.rs`) | any mapping within the violation bar | the same `rfi` bar, finer to coarser (both directions for a duplicate encoding). A near-unique column maps onto anything, and anything maps onto a near-constant column |
| categorical outliers (`surprises.rs`) | the context's binomial tail against the level's rate in the whole column was computed and used only to sort | stated only when the tail is below 0.05 divided by the number of contexts evaluated (Bonferroni); the others are counted in coverage |
| associations (`dep.rs`, `association.rs`) | stated when q ≤ 0.05; claim gave the "maximum measured effect" | also needs effect ≥ 0.1 (`MIN_EFFECT`). The claim names the statistic and its sign ("Spearman rho 0.423, the strongest of the four measured"). Pairs below 0.1 stay in `report.associations()` with tier `none` |
| order (`order.rs`) | "a ≤ b is violated on 5–25% of rows, the reverse on more" stated when the columns shared a scale, or were contained (both floored at zero, sometimes equal) | stated only when an exact decomposition makes the lower column a part of the upper one (the whole minus the part equals a sum of other columns). Certified orders (≤ 1% violations) are unchanged |
| level contrasts (`levels.rs`) | one statement per level; scale ratio of absolute IQRs, with an infinite ratio when the typical other level had no spread | one statement per (grouping, measure) when several levels differ: "cnt varies across hr: medians from 6 (hr = '3') to 475 (hr = '17'); 10 of 24 levels differ from their pooled rest", with every level's median and tests in the evidence `levels` table (form `profile`). No scale contrast when either IQR is 0. For a measure with no negative values, the scale ratio is of relative spread (IQR over median). The module doc now describes the comparison the code makes |
| panel entities (`pipeline.rs`) | any identifier or dimension with ≥ 10 values | also needs at least two non-null rows per entity on average. An order number has no history, and its "entity total" is one row's value |

## How the bars were set

The gate's 27 tables were profiled as they are and with every column shuffled independently (seed 0). On the shuffled tables every multi-column statement is false.

- **AFD `rfi`.** The largest value among stated approximate FDs on shuffled tables was 0.118, across 18 statements. On the unshuffled tables, statements with `rfi` below 0.18 are chance-level: "amount, people_liable → foreign_worker" (−0.016), "date_time → temp" (0.029, from a near-unique timestamp). Statements above 0.35 are mappings, such as filed and form → fiscal_year (0.81) and tax_amt → subtotal (0.57). No unshuffled statement lies between 0.18 and 0.35. 0.25 sits in that gap.
- **Rollups.** Every rollup stated on shuffled tables came from a column with one row per level (purchase_order_number to due_date, territory_id, …) or onto a 99.9%-constant column (traffic_volume to holiday). All of them have `rfi` near 0.
- **Categorical contexts.** On shuffled thyroid, "within t3_measured = false and tt4_measured = true, goitre is false in 99.6% of rows" holds where goitre is false in about 99% of the whole column. The tail against the column's rate is large, and the Bonferroni bar rejects it. Adult's "within relationship = Husband, marital_status is Married-civ-spouse in 99.9%" (46% overall) passes.
- **Effect floor 0.1.** An association below it explains under 1% of the other variable's variance. At 48,842 rows (Adult), every tested pair had q < 0.05, including r = 0.051.

## Capability removed

A bound that holds by construction and is broken in bulk (accepted suggestions exceeding generated ones on 24% of rows) is no longer stated. From the values alone it is the same shape as two differently scaled temperatures that cross both ways, and #374 measured the second case. Stating it needs the bound to be declared. There is no hint for a declared pairwise order, and adding one waits until the ceilings reach zero (`docs/agents.md`).
