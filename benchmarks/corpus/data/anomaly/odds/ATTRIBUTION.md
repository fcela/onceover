# ODDS — status (test-only, not wired)

  datasets, originally at Stony Brook's ODDS library.
- **Status**: not wired. The original distribution point is moribund;

  already in this corpus in primary form). Members will be admitted
  individually with their primary source and license, not via the
  suite.
